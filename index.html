Questions

Short answers to the things people ask first.

Everything you need to know about our loans, pricing, and process. No fine print, no surprises.

01 How much can I borrow?

Imaarika loans range from KES 5,000 to KES 15,000, sized to what your business can comfortably repay. We assess your daily cash flow to make sure the loan fits, not strains, your business.

02 How long do I have to repay?

Up to 30 days, with daily repayments. Daily instalments are small, so they fit the way market and shop income arrives. You can also repay early without any penalty.

03 What will it cost?

A KES 500 processing fee and 27% interest. Any penalties are stated up front, and there are no hidden charges. You see the full price before you accept, and you decide.

04 How fast can I get a decision?

Our target is two hours on a complete application. We never skip identity checks, affordability or approval, so an incomplete file takes longer. But we keep you updated at every step.
