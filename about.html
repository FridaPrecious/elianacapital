Why we exist

We lend with purpose, so businesses grow sustainably.

Our mission. To unlock opportunity through accessible, responsible and growth-oriented lending: a gateway to finance that is human-centred, transparent and matched to real business needs.

Who we serve. Micro and small business owners, informal-sector earners with genuine cash flow, and households needing short-term working capital. We are female-owned and female-run, and proud to back women entrepreneurs.

Our name

Eliana means light, and an answered prayer.

In Hebrew, Eliana means ‘My God answered’. In Greek, it points to light. For us it comes down to one idea: opportunity that responds to real need.

How we work

The standards every colleague helps deliver.

+Right first time

Right first timeSahihi mara ya kwanza

No file moves until identity checks, affordability and approval are complete. Our records are accurate, documented and ready to be audited. Speed never becomes a reason to skip a control.

+Respect earns repayment

Respect earns repaymentHeshima huleta marejesho

We price in plain language, with no hidden charges, ever. We follow up firmly and courteously and never intimidate a customer. We handle your data as carefully as your money.

+Own the outcome

Own the outcomeWajibika kwa matokeo

We know our targets daily and say where we stand. We raise arrears, errors and risks early, and we do what we said by when we said.

+Lift as you raise

Lift as you raiseInuka na uwainue wengine

We coach openly, share what is working in each market, and recognise good work out loud. Feedback is a tool, not a verdict.
